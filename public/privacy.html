Privacy Policy

Nostr District · Effective June 17, 2026

Nostr District is a browser-based application that runs entirely on your device. We keep no user accounts on our servers, and the app is designed so that we cannot read your private keys. This policy explains what data is involved and how it is handled.

1. Google sign-in & Google Drive

When you choose “Continue with Google,” the app requests a single, minimal Google permission: drive.appdata (the Google Drive Application Data folder).

  • This grants access to one hidden, app-specific folder in your own Google Drive — and nothing else. The app cannot see your email address, name, profile, contacts, photos, or any other files in your Drive.
  • In that folder the app stores one encrypted file: a backup of your Nostr private key.
  • The backup is encrypted on your device with a key derived from a password you choose (and, optionally, a passkey / biometric). Only ciphertext is uploaded. Neither we, Google, nor anyone with access to your Drive can decrypt it without your password or passkey. The file contains no name, public key, or other identifying information.
  • The app reads this file back only to let you log in again on the same or another device.

There is no server in this process — all encryption and decryption happen in your browser, and we never receive your Google data.

Limited Use disclosure

Nostr District’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use drive.appdata access solely to store and retrieve your encrypted account backup. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not allow humans to read it.

Revoking access & deleting your data

You can revoke the app’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the encrypted backup from your Drive’s app data (Drive settings → “Manage apps”), or with the app’s “erase and start new” option. Deleting it permanently removes the backup; if you have not saved your private key elsewhere, the account cannot be recovered.

2. Nostr data (public by design)

Nostr District is a client for the Nostr protocol. Content you publish — posts, profile, reactions, and similar — is sent to public Nostr relays and is, by the nature of the protocol, public and not controlled by us. Please don’t post anything you wish to keep private. Direct messages use end-to-end encryption (NIP-17 / NIP-44) where supported.

3. Other login methods

You may also sign in with a Nostr browser extension (NIP-07), a remote signer (NIP-46), your private key (nsec), or as a guest. With these, your key is handled by the extension or signer, or held only in your browser’s memory for the session; we never receive it.

4. In-app Lightning wallet

The app includes an optional Lightning wallet powered by the Breez SDK. Wallet keys are derived from your Nostr key and handled on your device. Lightning transactions are processed by the Breez / Spark network under their terms; we do not custody funds.

5. Multiplayer presence

To show players in shared spaces, the app connects to a real-time presence server. It relays ephemeral presence and position data, along with your public Nostr identifier (npub), to other connected players. This data is not persisted beyond the live session.

6. Analytics

We use privacy-respecting Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not fingerprint or track individuals across sites.

7. Cookies & local storage

The app stores preferences and session data (such as your chosen login method and theme) in your browser’s local storage. It does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Children

Nostr District is not directed to children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above reflects the latest version.
